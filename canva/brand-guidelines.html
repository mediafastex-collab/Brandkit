02 Logo

The FX mark

The F holds steady. The orange chevron drives forward through the X. Every diagonal runs 2 across for every 3 down (56°). Always use the official files and never redraw it.

Clear space

Keep 1x clear on every side, where x is the chevron's stroke width. Use 1.5x around lockups.

Minimum size

Mark 24 px / 8 mm. Badge 32 px. Horizontal lockup 140 px / 35 mm.

Backgrounds

Light: primary. Dark: reversed. Orange: one-colour black. Busy photos: add a dark overlay.

02 Logo

Don't change the mark

Don't

Stretch, squash or rotate it.

Don't

Recolour the chevron. It is always #FF6600.

Don't

Add shadows, glows or outlines.

Don't

Flip the chevron backwards.

Don't

Close or fill the gap between the chevron and the X.

Don't

Place it on low-contrast or busy backgrounds.

05 Colour

Fastex Orange

#FF6600 · RGB 255 102 0 · CMYK 0 60 100 0

Ink

#0D0D0D

Paper

#FBFAF8

Ember

#C94A00

Slate

#63636B

Proportion

Paper 58% · Ink 30% · Orange under 10%. If everything is orange, nothing stands out.

Contrast

Ink on orange (6.6:1) for buttons. Never white on orange (2.9:1) or orange text on paper (2.8:1). Use Ember for small orange text.
